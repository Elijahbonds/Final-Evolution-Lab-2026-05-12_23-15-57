# The Flip loop pack: design spec v1 (2026-09-25)

Owner decision #15 asks for a FEL-made loop pack (melodic loops, chord stabs, horn hits, voiced vocal chops) plus a
"FEL theme" for the first "chop this" lesson, each item with a written provenance record. The inspiration is a
producer chopping a theme on an AKAI pad. Today the Flip's only built-in sources are eight 808 one-shots, which chop
into clicks. This workflow makes the pack, and phase 5 wires it in. The file rules are CONTRACT.md; this file is the
design, the item list per producer, and the measured numbers behind the rules.

**The pack at a glance:** 3 theme candidates, 10 loops, 14 one-shots, 3 textures, 36 vocal chops, 3 chop sheets and
4 kit banks, 69 items in all. The budget is about 3.5 MB against the 8 MB cap. Everything is generated by numpy
and fel_synth, and the voices come from local Kokoro.

## 1. How the Flip hears a file

This is `Flip.ts onsetSlices`, exactly (see `onsets_check.py`). The file is mixed to mono and cut into 10 ms
windows, and each window's RMS energy is compared with a slow floor, which is an EMA of the past windows (0.9 /
0.1, about a 100 ms memory). An onset is a window whose energy is more than **2.2 × the floor** (and above 0.02),
at least 80 ms after the previous onset. The first 16 onsets become the pads, and each slice runs to the next onset.
With fewer than 2 onsets, the Flip makes 8 equal grid slices.

What this means for writing (every point was measured on fel_synth renders with the mirror, and checked
sample-exact against the app's real `Flip.ts`):

1. **The finder only hears hits.** A new event registers only if the energy just before it, averaged over about the
   last 100 ms, has fallen to about 40 % or less of the event's first 10 ms. A legato note, a note that re-strikes
   over its own sustain, or a melody over a loud held chord is invisible to it.
2. **Low end rules the RMS.** A sustained sub or bass under a melody was the whole problem in the first sketch: 16
   melody notes over a held EP chord and a sub gave **4 onsets** (the bar downbeats). A bass below about 70 Hz also
   ripples the 10 ms windows, which gives near-misses and spurious onsets. Themes have no sustained bass. A bass
   riff loop plays in octave 2 (E2 and up), staccato, with its high-pass at about 45 Hz.
3. **The bed must stay low.** A pad or chord bed at -14 to -16 dB against the chop events, high-passed at 200 Hz or
   more, with a slow attack (0.25 s or more) so it never makes an onset of its own, is fine. A bed at -10 dB costs
   about a third of the margin (table B).
4. **Decay must fit the spacing.** Table A gives the ratio the Flip sees for an equal-level next event when the
   previous event decays with an RMS time-constant τ and there is no background. Aim for 3.5 or more, so the finder
   still has margin after reverb and the bed. Rule of thumb: **τ ≤ IOI / 3**.

   | τ (s) \ IOI (s) | 0.20 | 0.25 | 0.30 | 0.40 | 0.50 | 0.67 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 0.06 | 6.2 | 9.5 | 15 | 40 | 109 | 633 |
   | 0.10 | 3.5 | 4.7 | 6.5 | 13.7 | 30 | 130 |
   | 0.15 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 3.8 | 6.5 | 11.6 | 33 |
   | 0.20 | 2.1 | 2.4 | 2.8 | 4.2 | 6.7 | 15 |
   | 0.30 | 1.7 | 1.9 | 2.1 | 2.7 | 3.7 | 6.3 |
   | 0.50 | 1.5 | 1.5 | 1.6 | 1.8 | 2.2 | 3.1 |

   Table B: the same with τ = 0.1 s over a constant background at -16 / -14 / -12 / -10 dB relative to the event
   peak. At IOI 0.4 s the ratio is 5.4 / 4.5 / 3.8 / 3.1.
5. **Gaps between notes.** Re-articulate every chop event. A note's gate should be at most about 60 % of the time to
   the next event. Lead phrases in fel_synth glide notes that touch into one legato segment (`legato_gap`), and a
   legato note never makes an onset.
6. **Reverb and echo.** Use a short room or plate send of 0.12 or less on the chop parts, and no delay echoes on
   them (an echo is either a spurious onset or a raised floor). A long hall is fine on a quiet bed.
7. **Chord size is loudness.** fel_synth normalises every note to the same peak, so a 5-note chord is about 7 dB
   louder than one melody note. Balance with `song.mix[stem].gain_db`. **fel_synth 1.0.0 never applies `gain_db`**
   (a library bug; the songs are levelled by loudness and limiting only). `flip_pack.render_loop` does apply it.
8. **Spacing on the grid.** Chop events need to be 0.15 s apart at the very least, and in practice 0.3 s or more (an
   8th at 100 BPM), unless the first one is a very short pluck (τ ≤ 0.1). A 16th pickup into a loud note is not a
   separate pad: leave it out of `suggestedPads` and play it 12 dB or more under.
9. **The window lock.** `flip_pack` places every attack at k × 10 ms + 1 ms, moving it by 5 ms at most, and moving
   everything on the same grid position together. So the finder cuts 1 ms before each attack at 44.1 and 48 kHz,
   and `onsets == suggestedPads` except for the first (0.01 vs 0.0; CONTRACT §12.1). At 100 and 125 BPM, 16ths are
   already whole windows, so nothing moves.

**Recipes measured with the mirror** (onsets found / events, with the lowest ratios):

| recipe | result |
|---|---|
| `pluck` melody (decay 0.18, gate 2/16) over a `pad` bed at -14 dB, HP 220, 90 BPM, mostly quarter IOIs | **16/16**, ratios 6 to 11 |
| `ep` melody note + `stab` chord together on each event, `pad` bed at -16 dB | 15/16; only the one 8th-note pair missed |
| `brass` melody (attack 6 ms, sustain 0.5, gate 2/16) | 15/16, 2.6 to 6, four near-misses; after `tame()` + `accent()`, 16/16 |
| `pluck` arp in 8ths, decay 0.12, 120 BPM, pad at -16 dB | **16/16**, 3.0 to 5.0 |
| `mallet` marimba in 8ths, decay 0.12, 125 BPM | **16/16**, 2.8 to 3.7 |
| `strings` art='stacc', sustain 0.1, gate 1/16, 112 BPM | **10/10** |
| `gtr` mute 0.9 in 8ths, 100 BPM (or quarters at mute 0.5) | **16/16** (8/8) |

| `bell` (decay 1.2), or any melody over a held chord + sub | 1 to 4 onsets: unusable as a chop source |

## 2. Tools

Use `/Users/elijahbonds/.cache/fel-kokoro/.venv/bin/python` (numpy, soundfile with MP3, kokoro-onnx 0.6.1). There
is no scipy and no ffmpeg, and nothing may be installed.

```python
import sys; sys.path.insert(0, '/Users/elijahbonds/Claude/outbox/finish-release/musicsuite/songgen')
import flip_pack as P          # the finisher (frozen)
import flip_voices             # registers flute / strings / gtr / mallet (frozen)
import voices_cypher           # optional: registers slapx / clavx / congax / kickx (the song lane's, read-only)
F = P.F                        # fel_synth: Song API, VOICES, voicing(), Key, midi(), StemMix, fft_filter, ...
```

* **Loops.** `song = P.loop_song(id, bpm=, key=, bars=, seed=, swing=0.5)` gives a fel_synth `Song` (1-based bars,
  sixteenths 0 to 15). Add parts with `song.note / chord / phrase / pattern / fx` on any of the eight stem names,
  which work as track names here. Mix with `song.mix[stem] = F.StemMix(gain_db, eq, drive, reverb, reverb_type,
  delay, pan, width, duck, tremolo)`. Then:
  * `y = P.render_loop(song)` renders one seamless stereo cycle, window-locked.
  * `pads = P.pads_from_song(song, 'lead', 'horns', drop=[...], extra=[...])` gives the suggested pads (the chop
    events).
  * `P.write_loop(song, y, kind='loop'|'theme', title=, tags=, suggested_pads=pads, script=__file__, extra={...})`
    writes the item.
  * Parameters starting with `_` are the finisher's: `_nolock`, `_nopad` (this event is not a pad).
* **One-shots.**
  * `P.render_voice(voice, pitch|None, dur, vel, seed=, **params)` renders one note.
  * `P.render_chord(voice, pitches, dur, vel, seed=, strum_ms=, **params)` renders a chord.
  * `P.process(x, eq=, drive=, reverb=, reverb_type=, seed=)` is a mono chain with a reverb tail.
  * `P.write_oneshot(id, x, kind='stab'|'hit'|'chop', title=, tags=, script=, seed=, root=, chord=, extra=,
    prov_extra=)` trims to the first transient, fades, sets the peak to -3 dBFS, encodes and validates.
* **Chop sheets.** Place each trimmed chop at `int(P.pad_time(t) * P.SR)`, then call `P.write_sheet(id, mono,
  bpm=, bars=, title=, tags=, suggested_pads=[P.pad_time(t) ...], script=, seed=, extra=, prov_extra=)`.
* **Textures.** `P.write_texture(id, stereo, title=, tags=, script=, seed=, loop=, bpm=, bars=, lufs_target=-22,
  clicks_by_design=False)`. For a seamless texture, render it longer than the loop and pass it through
  `P.fold(y, n)`.
* **Shaping** (detector-keyed; use only after fixing the arrangement):
  * `P.shape_chop(x)`: one onset per spoken chop.
  * `P.accent(y, pads, boost_db=3)`: a transient shaper on the pads.
  * `P.tame(y, pads, cap_ratio=1.8, max_cut_db=4)`: pulls non-pad rises under the finder.
* **Voice.** `P.resample(x, 24000)` takes Kokoro's 24 kHz output to 44.1 kHz. `P.varispeed(x, semitones)` is
  MPC-style pitch, where speed and pitch move together.
* **Look.**
  * `P.report(id)` shows the finder's view at 44.1 and 48 kHz.
  * `onsets_check.py audio/<id>.mp3` does the same from the command line.
  * `build_pack.py --check --partial --only <your id prefixes>` checks your items against the contract.
* **Dry run.** `FLIPPACK_DIR=<scratch dir>` renders anywhere else, and `strict=False` on a `write_*` records a
  failing item instead of raising.
* Every `write_*` prints `ok` or the failing rules plus warnings, and raises on a failure by default. It writes
  `audio/<id>.mp3` and `items/<id>.json`, and the sidecar carries the provenance (script, seed, sha256s, tools).

**New voices** go in your own module (`songgen/flip_<producer>_voices.py`), registered with `@F.voice(...)`, the
same way `voices_cypher.py` does it. Build them from sines, band-limited oscillators (`F.filtered_osc`,
`F.additive`) and seeded noise (`F.stft_noise`, `rng`). Never read an audio file.

## 3. Rules every producer follows

1. **Scripts.** Use one script per producer: `songgen/flip_themes.py`, `flip_loops.py`, `flip_oneshots.py`,
   `flip_chops.py`. Each one renders all its items, takes `--only id[,id]`, and ends with its self-check. Changing
   a script changes its `scriptSha256`, so re-render all of its items before you finish (the contract re-hashes
   them). The seeds are assigned below. Every random draw derives from the item seed (`F.rng_for(seed, ...)`), so a
   re-run is byte-identical on this machine.
2. **Stay in your lane.** Write only your own ids, scripts and bank files. `flip_pack.py`, `flip_voices.py`,
   `onsets_check.py` and `build_pack.py` are frozen. If one of them blocks you, work around it in your own module
   and say so in your report. Never write inside the repo.
3. **Done means:**
   * every item of yours passes `build_pack.py --check --partial --only <prefixes>` with no failures (warnings are
     allowed but should be explained);
   * the item counts and ids match this spec;
   * `onsets_check.py --parity <your files>` prints PARITY OK;
   * your report lists each item with its onset count, lowest ratio, LUFS or peak, bytes, and a two-line "what it
     sounds like", plus your IP check (what you compared each line against).
4. **Musical bar.** Each item should be one a player wants to chop: a clear identity in its first bar, pads that
   stand on their own when hit out of order, and harmony where any pad over any other stays consonant (chords that
   share two or more tones, modal vamps). The pads should be 0.25 to 1.0 s long.

## 4. Producers and items

### 4.1 Themes producer: `songgen/flip_themes.py` (3 items)

These are the three "FEL theme" candidates for the first "chop this" lesson; the owner picks one (`themeDefault`
stays null until then). Each is a 4-bar arrangement, **drum-less**, stereo, -16 LUFS, with a melody of clear note
attacks over a chord bed, built so that **one pad = one musical event**. Pads 1 to n played in order replay the
phrase, and a re-flip sounds like a new idea. Aim for 14 to 16 pads (the minimum is 12), mostly one event per beat
with two or three off-beat anticipations for swing, each pad 0.4 to 1.0 s. Leave 0.33 s or more between events, and
0.45 s or more after a sustained note.

The recipe that measured best: **every chop event = a melody note + a short chord hit on the same grid position**
(the chord colours the pad and doubles the attack), over a slow-attack bed at -16 dB (high-passed at 200 Hz or
more), with no bass part. A soft root can live inside the bed's lowest voice.

| id | title | character (the owner reads this) | bpm | key | seed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `theme_a_sunday_tape` | Sunday Tape | soulful: warm EP chords, a small horn section answering, late-night and hopeful | 90 (swing 0.54) | Eb major | `0x7E0A` |
| `theme_b_skyline` | Skyline Anthem | bright synth-brass anthem: stacked brass stabs, a heroic lead climbing to bar 4 | 100 (straight) | D major | `0x7E0B` |
| `theme_c_dust_strings` | Dust & Strings | dusty flute over plucked strings, cinematic and a little wistful | 96 (swing 0.56) | A minor | `0x7E0C` |

**Theme A, Sunday Tape.** The chords are a starting sketch; they only need to share tones. One possible sequence is
|Ebmaj9|Cm9|Abmaj9|Bb13sus4|, or pick your own four with common tones.
* The melody is the `ep` voice (bright 0.6, decay 0.3 to 0.4, gate 2/16 or less) in the Bb4 to Eb6 range.
* The chord hits are `ep`, rootless and 4 notes, strummed 6 to 10 ms.
* The horn answers are `brass`, 2-part thirds, with attack 0.006, sustain 0.5 or less, release 0.06 or less, and
  bright 0.6. They land on the "and" of 3 and on 4, in the melody's gaps, as separate pads.
* The bed is a `pad` or `strings` art='legato' at -16 dB.

**Theme B, Skyline Anthem.** A sketch is |D|Bm7|Gmaj7|A7sus4 → A|. At 100 BPM the 16ths already sit on the finder's

* The hits are `brass` (voices 4, bright 0.8, attack 0.006, sustain 0.45, release 0.05) doubled by `stab` (cutoff

**Chop sheets (3, kind `chop`, `sheet: true`, mono).** A sheet is one file with 16 syllables, one per beat, so
loading it in the Flip puts one word on each pad (the MPC way). Sheets are `chop_sheet_warm` (90 BPM), `chop_sheet_deep`
(96 BPM) and `chop_sheet_bright` (100 BPM), each 4 bars long, 16 pads, titled "Vox Sheet (warm)" and so on.
* The pad order is `hey · uh · yeah · go · let's · go · one · two · check · it · F · E · L · ooh · yeah · hey`.
  Multi-syllable words are split at their energy dip, each syllable on its own beat.
* Each syllable is `P.shape_chop` then `P.trim_oneshot(peak_db=-3, max_sec=0.45)` (0.40 at 100 BPM). Place it at
  `int(P.pad_time(beat * 60 / bpm) * P.SR)` and write it with `P.write_sheet(..., extra={'text': 'hey uh yeah ...',
  'voice': 'warm'})`.
* Measured: 16/16 pads with no weak onsets in two voices at 90 BPM.
