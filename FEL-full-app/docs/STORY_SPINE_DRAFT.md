# Story Mode + Evolution Garden — Narrative Spine (draft for confirmation)

Per the build prompt: these four items gate ALL hub/world code. Confirm or
amend before Phase 1 proceeds. (All flavor text is original; numbers TUNE.)

## 1. Player motivation (one sentence)

**"You arrived at The Circuit as a nobody with a homemade training log and
a chip on your shoulder — prove that evolution is earned through work, not
granted by pedigree."**

Every World Gate is someone telling you your method doesn't count; every
gate you clear is the method proving itself in public. Ties to the Final
Evolution identity: the player's PRQ growth IS the story — the game already
measures how you actually improve, so the narrative argues that measured,
honest growth beats hype.

## 2. The Rival

**Vane** — "The Prodigy." Everything the player earns, Vane was handed:
sponsored gear, a factory-bred companion, a highlight reel before a real
match. Their philosophy: *talent is selection; training is marketing.*
Vane isn't evil — they're the comfortable answer the Circuit already
believes, which makes them harder to beat than a villain.

Vane recurs across tiers in context-appropriate forms: a dunk-off they
concede was luck, a dojo rematch where they start studying *your* tape, a
Colosseum final where their factory companion meets your raised one.

**Aerial rivalry: YES** — Vane acquires a mount-capable companion one tier
before the player can, and the tier-4 story beat is a flight duel/chase
through the Circuit's vertical district (the player chasing on a borrowed
or newly-evolved mount). This makes flight a Camera Director cinematic
requirement (Phase 3), not a Phase 4 afterthought: the chase needs the
contest-preset push-in + pulse language at altitude.

## 3. The Evolution Garden's narrative role

The companion is the thesis made flesh. The player's PRQ improves
invisibly (numbers in a profile); the companion evolves VISIBLY — fed by
the same training events, its forms mirror the player's own path (a
combat-heavy player raises a fighter build; a precision player raises a
technician). When Vane scoffs at "grinding," the companion standing beside
you — evolved through dozens of sessions — is the rebuttal. Garden growth
and player growth are one curve rendered twice.

## 4. Flight as narrative gate

Flight is **gated story content, not convenience**:
- The upper tier of the Central Nexus hub (the Creator Loft's sky decks and
  the Rooftop Circuit) is unreachable on foot — World Gates there stay
  sealed until you own a mount-capable companion.
- The tier-4 Vane flight duel cannot trigger without it (chase beats need
  both parties airborne).
- Two gated World Gates (aerial time-trial, sky-dojo) open only post-mount.
Flight therefore sits LATE in the garden arc (evolve → mount-capable form),
which justifies Phase 4's mount system landing before Phase 5's Colosseum.
