# Ship Pass 4 — Stages, Movement, Menus, Wiring: the Full Picture (opened 2026-09-04)

Owner directive (verbatim): "lets do a pass for all of the stages being improved.
The movement systems being improved. Menus, wiring, full scope, full picture."

Owner decisions (2026-09-04, asked up front):
- **Venues:** BOTH — procedural props, crowds, lighting moods and skies on every
  baked map first; CC0 prop packs where a venue clearly needs them (each download
  named with source and size before it happens).
- **Bounds:** a NAVMESH generated offline from each venue's map mesh (a recast
  library; the exact package is named before it is added). The hand-typed clamps
  in ten modes retire.
- **Menus:** a CONSISTENCY pass — one shell for every mode (pause, results,
  controls card, phone join, venue preview), every mode reachable in three taps.
  No visual redesign.
- **Scope:** focused. The anatomy layer and the physics lesson stay with pass 3.

## Findings log
- **4 Sep, phase 1 done.** `scripts/full-picture.mts` writes `SHIP-PASS-4-MATRIX.md`: 24 registry modes × venue spec, kit builder, baked map, movement cores, camera preset, typed clamps, phone bridge, gauntlet, play sweep, concept lock, test scripts, file size. 40 gaps. The shape of them: only 3 of 18 enabled modes stand on a baked map (karate, tennis, 3v3) although 13 maps are baked — dunk, ones, carnival and dunk duel build a kit court next to an unused Venice court; derby, penalty and golf build a kit field beside baked park, stadium and links; skate, snowboard and surf run procedural worlds beside baked park, slope and break. Seven enabled modes have no camera preset (penalty, golf, carnival, mixedcombat, dunkduel, dance, threepoint); 9 carry typed clamps (36 in all); 4 enabled modes lack a shared movement core (football, surf, onevone, mixedcombat use mode-local movement). The matrix cannot see venues mounted by a shared base (the board modes delegate to BoardRunMode), so it under-reports the board modes' kit builders.
- **4 Sep, phase 2 opened.** The raw `gridiron.glb` and `sand-court.glb` carry no textures (the pipeline refuses them as grey soup), so football and volleyball keep procedural venues and get their props in the procedural pass. First wiring: the four basketball venue specs (dunk, ones, 3v3, carnival) all point at the scanned Venice court, whose placement was tuned so the painted baseline hoop sits under the rim at world origin — the same rim position every basketball mode uses.
- **4 Sep, the naked hero, twice explained.** After the swap the hero and every rival played bare-legged in the modes while the Closet dressed them. Two causes, both measured. (1) MakeHuman garment maps carry a low alpha (jean shorts 0.49, boots 0.21): Blender exported them BLEND, which rendered as near-transparent cloth, and a half cutoff discarded them — garments are OPAQUE now. (2) The anime ink outline is an inverted hull drawn with depth, and the body's hull buried anything tighter than the ink width — thin widths still speckled, a positive depth offset made it worse, a negative one (−12 / −48 units) pushes every hull behind the surfaces it wraps: cloth wins, the contour stays at the silhouette. Four more modes sit on their venue specs (karate VS, showdown, three-point, dunk duel); captures clean.
- **4 Sep, phase 2 approach fixed.** Two venue paths exist: a venue SPEC (ground with painted markings, props, crowd tiers, lighting, optional baked map) and the procedural VenueKit builders. The spec is the richer one and the maps hang off it, so the phase moves every mode whose gameplay geometry is its own (rims, goals, holes, mounds built by the mode) onto a spec sized to that geometry, kit as fallback. Done: dunk, ones, 3v3, carnival, karate VS, showdown, three-point, dunk duel. Staged: golf (60×90 green, holes at z 26–39), derby (70×90 diamond), penalty (50×70 pitch, goal line z 10.4), football rush (44×52). Kept procedural: skate, snowboard, surf, gymnastics, big air (their worlds ARE the gameplay) and mixed combat (its ring top is the floor) — these get scenery, sky and lighting moods in the procedural pass instead of a spec.
- **4 Sep, owner decisions on the two specifics.** (1) Bounds: `recast-navigation@0.43.1` (MIT; 330 KB JS + 2.0 MB WASM) enters as a DEV dependency only — each venue's navmesh is generated offline by a script and shipped as small polygon data, and the game constrains movement with its own polygon test; no WASM in the browser. (2) Props: six Kenney CC0 kits from the shorepine/kenney mirror — Nature (3.0 MB, 329 models), Mini Skate and Mini Arena (1.1 MB, 42), City Kit Suburban (2.6 MB, 40), Racing and Minigolf (3.8 MB, 238) — fetched by sparse clone into `~/Developer/FEL-swarm/tools/kenney`; only the props a venue uses are copied into the app, trimmed.

### 2026-09-04 — phase 2, venues: specs for the four field modes, CC0 prop dressing everywhere
- **Specs**: `golf_loop` (60×90 green), `derby` (70×90 diamond), `penalty` (50×70 pitch, goal line z 10.4) and `football_rush` (44×52 gridiron) joined VENUE_SPECS with map keys coastal-links / baseball-park / soccer-stadium. Golf, Baseball, Soccer and Football Rush now mount spec-first with the kit as fallback (same pattern as karate/dunk/ones). 12 of 16 mode venues are on specs.
- **Props**: `scripts/venue/curate-props.mts` copies 68 models (1.1 MB with the one texture atlas) from the six owner-approved Kenney kits into `public/models/props/<kit>/` with `manifest.json` recording CC0. `visual/VenueProps.ts` loads each model once per scene and instances it; `visual/venuePropSets.ts` holds ten placement tables (venice-court, dojo, links, ballpark, stadium, gridiron, skatepark, slope, surf-break, gym). Spec venues get theirs from `mountVenue` (NexusVenue, not the fenced NexusWebScene); the board runs and air sessions call the loader directly. Unlit kit materials are switched to lit PBR so they sit in the IBL.
- **Gate**: `venuePropSets.test.ts` pins every model file present and every placement outside its play area (the gym gantry failed it at the origin — moved behind the start).
- **Corrections after the first frames**: (1) the three field maps are ±15 m walkabout islands — derby and penalty play inside theirs and look right; the golf course runs to z 39, so `golf_loop` keeps the procedural green (map key removed) under a links daylight sky. (3) The scanned Venice court carries its own fence and crowd, so the court set is palms and lamps outside it. (4) Kenney nature models are ~1.5 m at unit scale: trees now 4.4–5.2×, bushes 3.4×. (5) Four more dead twins found the hard way: `BoardRunMode.ts` as well as the golf/baseball/soccer files; the registry serves `SkateRunMode` / `SnowboardSlalomMode` / `SurfBreakMode` and `precisionModes`.
