# Lab credits folded into the wallet — owner decision 2026-09-04 ("Fold LC into the wallet, go")

## Before
Three balances, not one system (`docs/ASSESSMENT-2026-09-04.md` §3): wallet coins/shards; lab credits as `PlayerProfile.labCredits`
mirrored by the double-entry `LedgerAccount/Transaction/Posting` (`lib/ledger.ts`, currency `LC`); legacy `PlayerProfile.shards`.
Five routes each moved LC with their own conditional update plus a `postLc()` audit row (arena lock/pay/refund, shop, lessons,
ladder, sessions). The header chip showed coins and shards; the shop and the arena spent a currency the chip never showed.

## After
- `Wallet.lc` (BigInt, CHECK >= 0 via `prisma/wallet-constraints.sql`) is the balance; `WalletCurrency` gains `lc`.
- ONE mover, `applyLc(db, …)` in `lib/wallet/wallet-service.ts`: idempotency key → conditional non-negative update →
  `WalletLedgerEntry(currency 'lc')` → `PlayerProfile.labCredits` written as a MIRROR for legacy readers → `postLc()` keeps the
  double-entry house book. Works inside a caller's transaction. `requireFunds` (default) rejects `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`
  atomically; `rejectReplay` (the arena lock) throws `REPLAYED_KEY` so a replayed stake aborts the whole transaction — the
  house-book rule `scripts/arena-tests.ts` pins.
- Converted to the mover: `lib/arena.ts` (lock / pay winner / refund), `lib/arena-rivals.ts` (house treasury top-up),
  `/api/shop/purchase`, `/api/education/complete`, `/api/ladder/results`, `/api/sessions` (session credits keyed by the
  session row), `/api/signup` and `lib/profile-service.ts` (welcome grant), `/api/arena/config` (reads the wallet).
  Reason codes with labels: WELCOME_GRANT, LC_MIGRATION, ARENA_ENTRY, ARENA_WINNINGS, ARENA_REFUND, SHOP_PURCHASE,
  LESSON_COMPLETE, LADDER_PRIZE, SESSION_CREDITS, HOUSE_TREASURY.
- Chip: `DualWalletChip` shows coins · shards · LC; `/api/v1/wallet` returns `lc`; the earn event carries `lc`.
- Migration: `scripts/migrate-lc-to-wallet.ts` (`--dry` to report) carries every profile's `labCredits` into its wallet with one
  `LC_MIGRATION` entry, idempotent, and refuses to finish if the totals differ. Exported as `migrateLcToWallet(db)` and called by
  `scripts/seed.ts` and `scripts/ensure-playtest-user.ts`, so seeded balances land in wallets. `scripts/apply-wallet-constraints.ts`
  applies the CHECKs after `db push` / `migrate deploy`.
