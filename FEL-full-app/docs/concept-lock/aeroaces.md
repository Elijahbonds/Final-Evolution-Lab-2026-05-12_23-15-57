# Concept Lock — Aero Aces

**Benchmark:** Diddy Kong Racing flyers, recorded in `AeroAcesMode.ts`: toy
planes, low arcade handling, balloons/items, stunts, and themed lap circuits.

**Mode id:** `aeroaces` · **Implementation:** `lib/babylon/modes/AeroAcesMode.ts`
· **Route:** `/play/aero-aces`

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Status |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Arcade flight: steer, climb, gas, brake, no sim stall tax | Locked |
| A2 | Lap circuit with gates/checkpoints and rivals | Locked |
| A3 | Balloon items: missile, mine, shield, boost, banana progression | Locked |
| A4 | Stunts pay boost and must vary to keep scoring | Locked |
| A5 | Toy-plane presentation with pilot and scarf | Locked |

## Deviations

This is not a flight-sim ring time trial. The defining mechanic is kart-racer
combat in the air.
