# Three videos — trailer, advertisement, announcement

**Written 2026-09-20 against the shipped product, not a roadmap.** Every number in these scripts was read out of
the code on the day they were written: 7 families / 33 modes (`lib/nav/families.ts`), 14 venues all playable
(`lib/game-data.ts`), 10 chapters / 91 lessons / 42 drills (`lib/education/course.ts`), 19 pathways across 9
disciplines (`lib/guidance/pathways.ts`), 6 creator boost cards (`lib/cards/boosts.ts`).

If you shoot these later and a number has moved, re-read it from the source rather than trusting this file.
`SHELF_MODE_COUNT` exists precisely because the Play page had been saying "twenty-eight" while the shelf carried
thirty-three.

**One thing to hold on to across all three:** the product's own thesis, from `lib/camp/curriculum.ts`, in the
owner's words. Nothing in these scripts should contradict it.

> You cannot test whether your plan for becoming what you want to be is working. That loop is years long.
> Training has a loop that closes in weeks. So we use training to practice the moves — set a goal, measure
> honestly, build a plan, execute through the stall, prove the delta — and then we move those same moves onto
> the thing you actually want.
>
> We are not claiming squats make you an architect. We are claiming that the person who knows how to run a plan
> through a plateau is the person who becomes an architect, and this is the fastest place to learn that.

## 3 — THE ANNOUNCEMENT · 3 minutes

**Job:** say what this is, everything it does, and why it exists — in that order, because the why only lands
once somebody believes the what.

**Format:** owner to camera, intercut with the product doing the thing being described. No music under the
mission section; let it be quiet.

### Act I — The problem (0:00–0:35)

> **To camera.** "I can tell you in about four minutes whether a kid is going to get hurt. I did it for a
> scholarship athlete without touching him — I just looked.
>
> The problem is that there are not enough of me, and the thing I am looking for is not complicated. It is a knee
> that caves. A heel that lifts. One foot that lands louder than the other.
>
> A phone can see all three."

*Intercut: the Mirror's skeleton locking on; the four checks going green and red.*

### Act II — What it is (0:35–1:50)

> "Final Evolution Lab is one app with two halves that are actually the same half."

**The games** — *montage under it*
> "Thirty-three modes across seven families, in fourteen places. Basketball, combat, boards, racing, the field
> sports, party games, and the creative rooms — music, dance, acting, story. Real controllers, or a phone as the
> pad. It runs in a browser. No download."

**The Mirror** — *screen running*
> "Your camera is a movement screen. Stand back, and it walks you through the stations — it tells you which way
> to turn, it only counts while it can actually see you, and it waits if you step out. At the end it scores what
> it found, names the one-sided findings first, and prescribes the corrective work.
>
> It runs entirely in your browser. The camera feed is never uploaded. And it tells you what it is estimating
> rather than pretending to measure muscle."

**The Playbook** — *chapters*
> "Ten chapters, ninety-one lessons, forty-two drills — the whole of the Neuro-Mechanic Playbook, taught a
> chapter at a time. The control panel, the joints, the jump, the landing, the reset, and a chapter written for
> parents. Free."

**Coaching** — *roster + invite*
> "Coaches get a roster, a programme builder that reads the movement screen, and one invite link that puts their
> plan on an athlete's phone."

**Fuel** — *kitchens*
> "Your meal prescription comes from your own movement screen — and a number you bought can never change it.
> That is enforced in the code, not in a policy."

**The card, and the economy** — *card + QR*
> "Everything you do becomes a card. Somebody scans yours, makes an account, and you both earn. Shards are earned
> — never sold — and they buy creator cards, kits and sessions."

**Pathways** — *the counsellor*
> "And it watches what you keep coming back to, across nineteen roads in nine disciplines, and names one next
> step. It never closes a door. It shows you the facts it read, so you can disagree with them."

### Act III — Why (1:50–2:45)

> **To camera. No music.**
>
> "Here is the part that matters.
>
> You cannot test whether your plan for becoming what you want to be is working. That loop is years long. You
> find out at thirty whether the thing you did at nineteen was right.
>
> Training has a loop that closes in weeks. Set a goal. Measure honestly. Build a plan. Execute through the
> stall. Prove the delta.
>
> So we use training to practise those moves — and then we move the same moves onto the thing you actually want.
>
> I am not claiming squats make you an architect. I am claiming that the person who knows how to run a plan
> through a plateau is the person who becomes an architect. And this is the fastest place on earth to learn
> that."

### Act IV — The ask (2:45–3:00)

> "It is free to start, it runs in a browser, and there is a dunk waiting for you sixty seconds from now.
>
> Go and find out what your body is actually doing."

*End card: crest, URL, `Free to start. No download.`*

## Production notes

**Claims to keep honest.** Every one of these has a matching guard in the code, and the video should not outrun it:

- "estimated", never "measured muscle activation" — the Mirror's copy rule (`brief §2.4`), enforced in
  `mirror-v2-tests.ts`
- "never uploaded" — true, the pose pipeline is entirely client-side
- "shards are earned, never sold" — `prisma/schema.prisma` says so and `lib/wallet` enforces it
- "a bought number cannot change your meal prescription" — enforced by test in `lib/cards/boosts.test.ts`
- "it never closes a door" — the pathways module's own stated rule, tested
- do **not** say "diagnose", "assessment" in a clinical sense, or imply injury prediction

**Footage to capture.** The dev routes render every mode without a session: `/dev/mode/<key>`. The venue artwork
is in `public/venues/` at 16 files. There is no captured gameplay footage in the repo — that is the one thing
these scripts need that does not exist yet.
